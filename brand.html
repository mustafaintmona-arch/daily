House style

So a message from anyone on the team reads like it came from the same firm.

Voice

Write like a sharp colleague who knows the numbers, not like a brochure.
Short sentencesOne idea each. If a sentence needs a comma to survive, split it.
Lead with the number“Elora 3BR, 2.75M, 1,261 per sqft” beats “a wonderful opportunity has become available”.
One soft call to actionNever two. Never a hard close.
Urgency only when it is realA rival offer, a payment date, a launch closing. Never manufactured.
Warmth is finebro, brother, inshallah, Alhamdulillah are natural in team and warm-client messages. Keep them out of formal documents and first contact.
Never invent a numberIf a figure is an estimate, say estimate. If the market is flat, say flat. Name a weakness before the client finds it.

Body and labels
System sans
Body text, labels, buttons. Section labels are small, uppercase, letterspaced about 0.16em, in muted grey. All figures use tabular numerals.

PercentagesOne decimal only when the decimal matters

Formats

WhatsApp to a clientFour to eight lines. First line says what it is and the price. Middle gives the two or three facts that matter. Last line is one question. No greeting paragraph, no sign off.
Instagram captionTwo to four lines. One number, one reason it matters, one line of curiosity. Never a price list.
Listing pitch to an ownerOpen with the specific comparable sale near them: unit number, month, price. Then what you can do. Then one question.
Team messageDirect, more informal, still numbers first.
PDF one-pagerSerif title, gold rule, small uppercase gold section labels, two-column figure table, a plain-English paragraph near the end, then the footer.
